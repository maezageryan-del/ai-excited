An entry for the [ai-excited-01](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun): a full ocean liner — white hull, rows of portholes, red-and-black funnels — erupting out of a small clawfoot bathtub in an ordinary tiled bathroom. The porcelain is splitting, water and soap foam are exploding outward, and the wall tiles have cracked from the force of it. At the lower left a wet hand grips the rim of the tub, holding on as it happens; at the lower right a tiny yellow rubber duck rides the foam, perfectly calm. The whole scene is frozen at the loudest instant: the largest thing on the sea, breaking out of the smallest room in the house.
